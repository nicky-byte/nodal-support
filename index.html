Common questions

Does Nodal work without an internet connection?

Yes, once a model is downloaded. Downloading needs a connection; chatting and generating images after that do not.

Where do my chats and images go?

They stay on your iPhone. Nodal has no server and no account, so there is nowhere else for them to go. See the privacy policy.

Which model should I pick?

Every model in the app lists its size and what it is built for before you download it. Smaller models answer faster and use less storage; larger ones write better. If you are unsure, start with the one at the top of the list.

A model is taking up too much space.

Settings shows how much storage your models are using. Free up space removes every downloaded model at once; you can download the ones you still want again afterwards. Manage models opens the model list, where you can see what each one needs before downloading it.

Why was my image prompt refused?

Prompts are screened on the device. Requests for sexual content, anything sexualising minors, imagery of real people who have not consented, and graphic violence are refused, and that is not adjustable. If an ordinary prompt was refused by mistake, please email us with the wording so we can fix it.

I paid but the app is still locked.

Open the app and tap Restore at the top of the purchase screen, using the same Apple Account you bought with. If that does not work, email us.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
