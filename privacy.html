Nodal Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026

Nodal is an offline AI app for iPhone. It runs language models and an image generator directly on your device. There is no Nodal account, no Nodal server, and no analytics.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not operate a server that receives your data, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code of any kind.

What stays on your device

  • Everything you type, and every reply a model writes.
  • Every image you generate, and the prompts you used.
  • Any photo you attach to a conversation.
  • The models you download, and your settings.

All of it is stored inside the app's own container on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also clear chats and delete downloaded models from inside Settings at any time.

Network connections

Nodal connects to the internet for exactly two things, and neither carries anything you have typed:

  • Downloading models. When you choose a model, the app requests it from Hugging Face. As with any website you visit, Hugging Face can see the request and your IP address. Your prompts are never included.
  • Purchases. Buying or restoring is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details.

Once a model is downloaded, chat and image generation work with the network switched off.

Who has access

No one. Because nothing leaves your device, there is nothing for us, or any third party, to access, sell or share.

Children's privacy

Nodal is rated 18+ and is not directed at children. The models it runs are third-party open-source models whose output is not controlled by us, which is why the app is limited to adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect information at all.

Generated images

Image prompts are screened on your device before anything is generated, and requests for sexual content, sexual content involving minors, imagery of real people who have not consented, and graphic violence are refused. Generated images are saved only to your device unless you choose to share them. If you believe the app produced something it should not have, please report it using the Report button on the image screen or by emailing us.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, the CCPA and similar laws you have rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to return or erase. Data on your device is yours and under your control.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date, and the change will ship with an app update.
